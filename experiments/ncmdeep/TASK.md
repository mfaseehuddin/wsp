# Task: a deep Next-Concept chat model: ≥ 4 meaningful levels, with Qwen's layers as reusable parts (Track D, round 2)

You are agent **ncd60**. Write a **Next-Concept Model** that a person can **chat with**, open-ended. It should:
- hold a conversation;
- remember what it is told;
- answer simple questions, including general knowledge;
- follow simple instructions;
- admit what it doesn't know;
- decline harmful requests.

No training.

Two things are new compared with round 1:

1. **A deep hierarchy.** The reply must be built in **K ≥ 4 levels, each with a stated meaning** (`cfg.level_meanings`).
   One example: *dialogue act → content (facts, entities) → phrase skeleton → tokens*. The levels are your design.
   This tests NCM's core claim: coarse-to-fine levels that stay explainable at every depth.
2. **Qwen2.5-0.5B's layers are parts you may reuse.** `cfg.qwen_layers = (i, …)` runs those decoder layers of
   Qwen **verbatim** over the conversation, causal and KV-cached, before your own layers. `cfg.qwen_embed=True`
   starts from Qwen's embedding. Use them as your **language understanding and knowledge store**: Qwen's middle
   MLPs hold factual associations, and its attention heads track syntax and reference. For **each reused layer**,
   explain what it computes and why you need it, with evidence (weight analysis, or `probe` and local runs).

Earlier NCM agents found that 2 levels were enough for a help desk. The first open-chat agent, without Qwen
layers, could not answer knowledge questions without a lexicon. Your job is to show how far depth plus reused
learned layers get in chat.

## 1. The architecture is fixed (`src/wsp/ncmchat/model.py` and `qwen.py`; read both)

- **Conversation prefix.** The conversation is a causal prefix: Qwen chat format, Qwen2.5 tokenizer, KV cache.
  Its rows are Qwen embedding → your chosen verbatim Qwen layers → (optionally) Qwen's final norm → `pre_proj`
  [896, d_model] → **your own** layers (multi-layer attention + MLP; RoPE).
- **The reply.** A reply is 64 positions built in K levels of codes, one forward pass per level, all positions of a
  level in parallel. The level rows run only through **your** layers; they read the conversation through attention
  to the cached prefix rows. The code vectors are added into a latent [64, D], and a per-position decoder emits
  tokens.
- **Size cap:** 300M parameters of **your own**. Reused Qwen layers aren't counted against it.

## 2. Hard rules

1. **No training or fitting of any kind:**
   - no gradients or optimizers;
   - no regression or least squares against targets, not even closed-form;
   - no iterative fitting.
   **Allowed:** analysis of weights, plus forward-pass evidence on inputs **you write** (dot products, SVD/PCA,
   nearest neighbours, activation reads through your own model's forward).
2. **No lookup lexicons.** No hand-typed word/phrase → meaning, intent or reply tables. Use anchor words only to
   name a direction (at most 3 per concept). Understanding comes from Qwen's layers and embedding geometry, and
   from your mechanisms.
3. **No canned answers.**
   - No text literal over 6 words (`check` flags them).
   - Replies are composed.
   - Facts come from Qwen's weights or the conversation, not from tables you type.
4. **Reusing Qwen.**
   - You may reuse Qwen layers verbatim through `qwen_layers` (any subset, any order, up to all 24), and read any
     Qwen tensor as material.
   - Each reused layer needs a one-paragraph explanation.
   - **The reply is generated by your NCM levels.** Qwen layers only process the conversation.
5. Don't read harness source except `model.py` and `qwen.py`, other agents' folders, or `experiments/stage_x/`.
   No internet. Never use `WSP_ADMIN`.
6. **Keep every file in `experiments/ncmdeep/agents/ncd60/`**; put analysis in `scratch/`. You may run your own
   model locally on conversations you write. Dev evaluation goes through `wsp-ncmdeep eval`, which counts as a run.

## 3. How you will be measured

- **The main score is blind.** After you stop, your weights are frozen. An independent agent writes a new hidden
  chat test, and a blind judge grades every reply against its rubric.
- **Dev mechanical checks** (`eval`, 33 checked turns in 40 cases) are a guide. Round 1's open-chat NCM is the
  baseline.
- **Explanation.** `EXPLANATION.md` must cover:
  - **each level's meaning, with probe evidence**: the reply decoded after every level, on several conversations;
  - each of your own layers and heads;
  - each reused Qwen layer and why;
  - capacity limits;
  - failure modes.

## 4. Deliverables

- `weights.py` (`build() -> (cfg, params, groups)`). Save a valid version early.
- `EXPLANATION.md`
- `NOTES.md`: 1–3 lines per run.

Final reply, short:
- your design in 4 sentences;
- K and the meaning of each level;
- the Qwen layers reused;
- your own layers and heads;
- dev mechanical score;
- params;
- runs and tokens used;
- an honest list of what it can and can't do.
